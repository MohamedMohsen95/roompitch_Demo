AI providers for floor plans, 3D models and text

Which API to use for image models (FLUX, Nano Banana), image-to-3D models (Tripo, TRELLIS) and basic LLMs, with list prices, plans and free tiers. Prices are list prices in USD read from each provider's pricing page this week; they move often, so check before you commit a budget.

Recommendation

  1. Runware is a good fit as the main provider. It is the only provider here with all three categories (FLUX and Nano Banana images, Tripo and TRELLIS 3D, and LLMs) behind one key and one bill. It also has the cheapest TRELLIS.2 we found ($0.0077 a model). Billing is pay-as-you-go with no plans, and new business-email accounts get $2 free.
  2. Keep Cloudflare Workers AI for the free tier. It is what RoomPitch already uses. It gives 10,000 neurons a day free (FLUX.2 klein images and the Llama 4 Scout and Moondream vision models), which is enough for demos and development at no cost.
  3. Call Google directly if Nano Banana becomes the main cost. Google's own Gemini API is the cheapest Nano Banana source ($0.0336 per 1K image for Nano Banana 2.1), and its Batch API halves that again. Resellers charge the same or more.
  4. No provider gives free 3D generation for production. The only free routes are signup credits ($1–2) and running TRELLIS yourself (it is open source). Budget about $0.01 for a TRELLIS.2 model and $0.20–0.30 for a Tripo model.

Free tiers, in short

Lasting free tiers

  • Cloudflare Workers AI: 10,000 neurons a day, resets at 00:00 UTC, no card. Covers FLUX.1 schnell and FLUX.2 klein images and Llama/vision LLMs. No Nano Banana, no 3D.
  • Google Gemini API: free text models (Flash, Flash-Lite). Image models are not on the free tier.
  • Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter: free LLMs with daily caps.

Signup credits

  • Runware: $2 with a business email, no card (about 260 TRELLIS.2 models or 60 Nano Banana 2.1 images).
  • WaveSpeed: $1, no card; some premium models are excluded.
  • fal, Replicate: signup credits reported by third parties; amounts not on their pricing pages.
  • Hugging Face PRO: $2 a month in credits for $9 a month.

Free 3D, honestly

  • No provider offers free 3D generation beyond signup credits.
  • TRELLIS and TRELLIS.2 are open source: free to run on our own GPU, or on Hugging Face Spaces (ZeroGPU quotas, fine for testing, not for customers).

Before deciding

Check with Runware

  • Nano Banana 2.1 per-image price (listed only by input tokens).
  • LLM output-token prices (only input shown).
  • What TRELLIS.2 costs above resolution 512, and Tripo with textures.
  • Volume pricing at our expected monthly spend.

Things to test, not just price

  • Speed: time to a finished 3D model, since users wait for it.
  • Output formats: GLB with PBR textures for three.js.
  • Request size limits, for plan images up to 3 MB.
  • Data use: free tiers (Google especially) may train on inputs.
